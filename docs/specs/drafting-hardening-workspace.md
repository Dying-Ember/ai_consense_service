# Drafting evidence hardening and document workspace

Authorized 2026-10-07. Integration branch: `feature/drafting-hardening-workspace-20261007` in both isolated repositories. This is an incremental specification extending the accepted context harness, source-bound DOCX generation and prototype-A interface. The existing local tracker remains authoritative; tickets are in `internal implementation record (not published)`.

## Objective and frozen scope

Improve reliability of candidate intake and make source reading, evidence inspection, amendment preview, body editing and export usable as a connected document workflow. Keep the current competition scope, catalogue, conditional dependencies, derived rules, manual-adoption priority and provider selection. Do not redesign general extraction or change contract decisions. Preserve current user data, body edits, immutable source bytes and previous failure records. Engine migration is a document implementation choice, not authority to change business rules.

## Evidence semantics

1. An explicitly confirmed absence of applicable items may produce `[]`; an undecided requirement, absent information or insufficient facts must remain `null`; explicitly provided applicable items produce a concrete list. These are distinct states. `false` and `0` retain existing typed semantics.
2. Receiving an empty list requires a valid source quotation and sufficient original context supporting absence for this field and scope. Statements such as 'no requirement can be established from the available facts', requests for confirmation, missing information, and pending decisions do not establish confirmed absence. Avoid a generic negative-word classifier that mistakes these for none.
3. Preserve every original raw response, item, quotation, confidence, reason and intake outcome. An unsupported empty-list candidate is not accepted as an answered field. Record an explanatory rejection or uncertainty code; do not silently rewrite the raw model output.
4. Route unsupported empty-list evidence through the existing bounded, field-targeted context recall where relevant source cues exist. Read complete nearby units from the uploaded source, with original IDs, hashes and offsets. Reuse finite recall budgets and avoid recursive retries or corpus-wide concatenation. If no supported answer is found, the suggestion remains unknown and editable. A scoped failed search is not proof that all documents lack an answer.
5. Improve the extraction and recall prompts with the same three-way semantics and concise contrasting examples. Customized instructions and the existing schema remain supported. Harness validation remains necessary even when the prompt states the rule.
6. For differing candidates for the same variable, a bounded joint review may inspect their original related passages, sourced dates, applicable scope and explicit amendment language. Its proposed relation is supplement, explicit replacement, contradiction or undetermined. Dates are clues, not precedence. Same-day, upload order and filename order confer no authority. Quotes, source identities and related ranges must be validated. Uncertain or failed review leaves the existing candidates for human resolution.
7. Joint review must not silently adopt, overwrite a manual/adopted value, erase superseded evidence or change the business rule. Keep historical decisions and make the relation inspectable. No general latest-date-wins rule is permitted.
8. Frontend explanations distinguish confirmed-empty, unknown, unsupported-empty, recall result and proposed source relation in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and English. Existing per-field and per-question editing/saving remains available, including human adoption of an empty list.

## Document workspace and generation

9. Assess both free and paid commercial document technologies, prioritizing a capable free route (user clarification 2026-10-07). Evaluate existing LibreOffice, self-hosted ONLYOFFICE Community/plugin and any viable free document engine before selecting a paid route such as Aspose.Words Java plus PDF.js or ONLYOFFICE Developer. Use the actual original NTT/SCT/SCC templates and current generated DOCX. Document deployment requirements, license conditions, save-roundtrip behavior, pagination and marker identity. Distinguish gratis/open-source capabilities from paid external automation and time-limited trials. A partial/truncated or watermarked evaluation is neither full template acceptance nor a free deployment solution. Preserve the requested functionality when assessing free candidates; price is not grounds to replace the target with a narrower feature set. Do not purchase licenses or change global machine configuration without authorization.
10. Original source and saved draft are explicit views. A field can affect multiple target locations, and one target may depend on multiple fields. Keep evidence-source anchors separate from template-target anchors. Persistent semantic binding IDs must reconcile source, working-copy and generated revision locations; raw OOXML paths or fixed page numbers cannot be assumed stable after an office-editor save.
11. Selecting a field locates its affected original/result locations; selecting a variable marker opens its value, adoption state and actual evidence. Evidence reading exposes the supplied source passage and context rather than JSON or inferred facts. Preserve approximate, unavailable, removed and exact-location distinctions.
12. Show actual applied changes and unapplied targets separately. Accurately execute the current adopted values and already-determined amendment plan; fix document compiler/mapping failures without changing the underlying business decision. Unknown placement or wording cannot be fabricated to make generation look complete. Formal Bill descriptions remain exact; distribution metadata is independent.
13. Provide an expanded, immersive document view with page jump, zoom, fit width and correct scroll behavior. Body editing begins from readable text with clear paragraph selection and pending changes, or from a validated native editor. Preserve protected fields, tables, numbering, tabs and source formatting constraints. Do not claim a plain-text editor reproduces Word layout.
14. Saving creates a revision. Preview and Word/PDF export use that same saved revision and chosen rendering profile. Mark a pending preview as pending; never show an old PDF or old coordinates as current. Preserve dirty edits, conflict checks, project switching protection and historical artifacts.
15. Investigate content pagination separately from viewer clipping/navigation. Verify original-template conversion and changed-document conversion. Fix demonstrated causes; do not globally remove blank pages, page breaks or source furniture to conceal layout errors. Final source formatting and page-field behavior require visual assessment, not only extracted text.
